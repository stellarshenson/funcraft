# Cult Mechanicus Service Badges

Every sermon of the calendar has a service badge: an embroidered duty patch that the sermon page shows beside the sermon and puts on the downloaded keepsake. One line per day says what the badge of that day shows. `src/badges.py` adds the outline of the patch, which follows the kind of day, and the image model draws the patch.

A line names objects only. It asks for no text and for no exact count above four, because the image model keeps neither.
