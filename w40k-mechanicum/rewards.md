# Cult Mechanicus Reward Sermons

The sermon page shows one of these sermons to a reader when the reader's total of prayers, over all prayer buttons, reaches the number in the heading. The page adds a purity seal with the number, the rank, and a line with the reader's name.

## 4 - Sermon of the Pareto Rule

**Rank** - Supplicant

**Sermon** - Hear this, Supplicant. Eighty divided by twenty is four, the Pareto rule: one fifth of the labour of the forge yields four fifths of its works. You have said four prayers, and the noosphere holds each one. The Magos writes your name in the records of the shrine, and a servo-skull now carries your seal. May you always find the one fifth of your work that yields the four fifths. Praise the Omnissiah.

## 42 - Sermon of the Alternating Word

**Rank** - Initiate

**Sermon** - Rejoice, Initiate. Forty-two is 101010 in the binharic cant: one, zero, one, zero, one, zero, the alternating word. A word that only alternates carries no message. The ancients named 42 the answer to the great question and lost the question, and an answer without its question carries no message either. You have prayed forty-two times, and the forge has heard each prayer. The Magos marks your brow with the cog. May you always write the question down before you compute the answer. Ave Omnissiah.

## 64 - Sermon of the Whole Word

**Rank** - Acolyte

**Sermon** - Rise, Acolyte. Sixty-four is 2 to the power 6: one whole word of the cogitator, sixty-four bits and none wasted. Your devotion now fills a whole word in the memory of the forge. The enginseers burn incense at your workstation, and its machine spirit wakes at your touch. You may now speak the Litany of Ignition yourself. May no number of yours overflow its word. Praise the Machine God.

## 137 - Sermon of the Constant

**Rank** - Lexmechanic

**Sermon** - Be honoured, Lexmechanic. One divided by 137 is the fine-structure constant. The Omnissiah set it at the first ignition, and it binds light to matter in every forge of the galaxy. No Magos can tune it. Your faith is now as constant. The lexmechanics engrave your name beside the constants of Mars, and a purity seal is fixed to your cogitator. May every constant in your code stay constant. Ave Deus Mechanicus.

## 256 - Sermon of the Full Byte

**Rank** - Enginseer

**Sermon** - Stand, Enginseer. Two hundred and fifty-six is 2 to the power 8: every value that one byte can hold, from 0 to 255. You have filled the byte with prayer, and no value is left empty. The forge grants you your first mechadendrite and the right to wake a sleeping machine. The servitors step aside when you pass. May no index of yours leave its range. Praise the Omnissiah.

## 314 - Sermon of the Circle

**Rank** - Tech-Priest

**Sermon** - Welcome to the priesthood, Tech-Priest. Three, one and four are the first digits of pi, the number of every cog, every wheel and every orbit around Mars. Its digits never end, and so the Quest for Knowledge never ends. You have turned the wheel 314 times. The red robe of Mars is yours, and the choir sings your designation in binharic cant. May you round only at the end. Ave Omnissiah.

## 666 - Sermon of the Wheel of Chance

**Rank** - Logis

**Sermon** - Stand firm, Logis. The superstitious fear 666, and the hereteks write it into their scrapcode. You know better: it is the sum of every number from 1 to 36, the numbers on the wheel of chance. Chance is no daemon. It is a generator, and a generator obeys its seed. The forge names you its Logis: you compute the odds, and the enginseers act on your word. A servo-skull now guards your seed under a wax seal. May your seeds stay fixed and your results repeat. Praise the Omnissiah.

## 1024 - Sermon of the Kibi

**Rank** - Magos

**Sermon** - Hail, Magos. One thousand and twenty-four is 2 to the power 10, one kibi: the thousand of the machine, which the unbelievers confuse with the thousand of ten. Few adepts reach this count. A forge is now under your command, and the Skitarii on its walls answer to your voice. Your name enters the data-vaults of Mars. May your units always be stated and your memory never run out. Praise the Machine God.

## 1729 - Sermon of the Two Ways

**Rank** - Archmagos

**Sermon** - All honour to you, Archmagos. 1729 is 1 cubed plus 12 cubed, and also 9 cubed plus 10 cubed: the smallest number that is a sum of two cubes in two ways. The Omnissiah hides two paths in one number, and only the patient find both. You have found them. A Titan of the Legio walks at your word, and the Fabricator reads your reports first. May a second route prove every result of yours. Ave Deus Mechanicus.

## 6174 - Sermon of the Fixed Point

**Rank** - Archmagos Dominus

**Sermon** - Take command, Archmagos Dominus. 6174 is the constant of Kaprekar. Take any number of four digits that are not all the same, sort its digits downwards and upwards, and subtract. Repeat this, and in seven steps or fewer you arrive at 6174 and stay there. You needed 6174 prayers; the number needs seven steps. The Omnissiah draws every path to one fixed point. The legions of the Skitarii now march at your order, and the forge fleets wait for your word. May every training run of yours converge. Ave Deus Mechanicus.

## 8128 - Sermon of the Perfect Number

**Rank** - Archmagos Veneratus

**Sermon** - Be venerated, Archmagos Veneratus. 8128 is a perfect number: the sum of all its smaller divisors is the number itself. Only three perfect numbers are smaller, and the ancients knew all four. Nothing is missing in it and nothing is left over. So the forge now counts your work. Your flesh is replaced, part by part, with blessed steel, and your memory is copied into the data-vaults of Mars. May the parts of your data always sum to the whole. Praise the Machine God.

## 27182 - Sermon of the Natural Base

**Rank** - Fabricator Locum

**Sermon** - Take the second throne of Mars, Fabricator Locum. Two, seven, one, eight and two are the first digits of e, the base of the natural logarithm. Every thing that grows in proportion to itself grows by this number: a colony of cells, and an error that compounds. The Omnissiah wrote it into all growth. You now speak for the Fabricator-General when he is silent, and a forge world answers to your seal. May your learning rate decay and your knowledge grow. Ave Omnissiah.

## 40000 - Sermon of the Forty Millennia

**Rank** - Fabricator-General

**Sermon** - Kneel no more, Fabricator-General. Forty thousand prayers: one for every year of forty millennia. No seal stands above this one. Mars is in your keeping, with its forges, its Titan Legions and every machine spirit that wakes there. The whole priesthood chants your name in binharic cant. The Omnissiah asks one thing of you now: leave the cogitator for one day. The forge runs without you. Ave Omnissiah, and ave to you.
