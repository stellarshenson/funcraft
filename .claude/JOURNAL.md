14. **Task - Text follows the sheet's visible warp**: Added the edge-wave correction to the sheet chart in `w40k-mechanicum/src/inscribe.py` and wrote the general rule into `DESIGN.md` and the `design-w40k` skill<br>
    **Result**: Trigger: Star Colonel accepted the draped text on scriptorium book pages, rejected it on street parchments, and ordered the difference understood and generalised. Cause: MoGe-2 measures those parchments as a plane (bend 4-8 degrees) although they are drawn warped, so the chart was a plane and the Latin a straight block. Rule now: shape evidence is the measured surface plus the object's outline; a sheet is cut straight, so a wavy edge on the flat chart is unresolved warp. `waves()` fits a line to each edge, bridges notches deeper than 8 % of the sheet's size, smooths to slope 0.35 at most, and blends the four waves into the chart. Sheet mask is the whole SAM sheet, not the text box. Nine scenes rebuilt, test frames checked, street frame accepted. 30 of 34 sheets carry Latin. Rule stated in `DESIGN.md` section 2.5 and the skill.
