2. **Task - Exact Latin on drapes and banners**: Stitched gold Latin on plain velvet in `src/gentextures.py`<br>
    **Result**: Trigger: Star Colonel asked why the drapes carry no Latin, theme "sanctified calculations". Z-Image-Turbo misspells Latin, so text is drawn in PIL over the generated cloth. `SANCTA` holds 12 phrases, `LATIN` maps them to banners, drape and hangings. `plain_rows` finds the plain span between gold borders (border column: gold fraction >= 0.42, span at least w/3; the first threshold 0.3 returned an empty slice because centre columns exceed it). `stitch` draws Liberation Serif Bold with satin ridges, emboss, couching cord and worn stitches, and returns the updated gold mask. Hangings have no plain span and stay unlettered.

3. **Task - Ten scene plates**: Generated ten 2432x768 scene plates with depth in `src/genplates.py`<br>
    **Result**: Trigger: Star Colonel asked for five very different scenes from the reference images, then five more. Z-Image-Turbo (9 steps, guidance 0) drafts several seeds per scene; `PICKS` records the chosen one. `inscribe` writes exact Latin on each parchment: pale mask from an adaptive value threshold and saturation < 205, cleaned sheet by normalised blur, Liberation Serif Italic, red initials after a full stop. Fixes on the way: every word got a red initial (index -1 wrapped), a long first word left tags blank (now force-fitted), text leaked onto a candle (box moved). MoGe-2 gives depth per plate; `src/backdrop.py` now places sky pixels on a far dome at twice the maximum depth.

4. **Task - Plate scene animation**: Built `src/scenes.py` and `src/render-scenes.sh`, rendered GIFs 04 to 13<br>
    **Result**: Each plate becomes a MoGe-2 depth mesh with an emission material and a per-pixel gain, seen by a camera drifting on a closed path. Flames flicker by looping 4D noise (a circle in z and W, so the loop closes), status-light cells switch per tick through white noise, a glint sweeps the saint's halo, the reliquary fans turn by UV rotation, dust motes float near the lens. `render-scenes.sh` runs scenes one after another on one GPU and assembles each GIF (1140x360, 224-colour palette). `blur()` failed on RGB arrays until its padding followed `a.ndim`.

5. **Task - Smoke flicker and loop speed**: Replaced scattering smoke with emission smoke and slowed all plate loops<br>
    **Result**: Trigger: Star Colonel found the animations too fast and saw light flickering off the smoke. Cause: scattering volume was noisy at 64 samples and the volume denoiser gave a different blotch pattern each frame. Smoke is now emission plus absorption, lit by the plate blurred 40 px and looked up through Window coordinates, so no sampling noise exists; frame-to-frame change fell from 9.16 to 2.57. Loop went from 40 to 80 frames (4 s), tick 16, camera amplitude 0.012 to 0.008 of nearest depth, flicker radius 2.5 to 1.5. All ten scenes re-rendered: seam 0.00, GIFs 22.6-25.9 MB.

8. **Task [Extended] - Rigged priests in plate scenes**: Priests of foundry and choir became skeleton-driven meshes; molten metal flows<br>
    **Result**: Trigger: a first version warped the plate per frame (measured motion matched command, r 1.00) and Star Colonel rejected it: figures are 3D meshes and must move by skeleton, not by warping. New pipeline: `src/cleanplate.py` removes priests and carried things with `ZImageInpaintPipeline` (seed drafts in `assets/gen/`, foundry seed 2, choir seed 3) and measures the result with MoGe-2, scaled to the original depth. Two faults fixed there: the hole included the molten streams and the prompt word "molten" painted glowing pools, and the painted background placed a pillar nearer than priest 7, hiding it, so backdrop depth is now pushed behind every actor's back. `src/actors.py` bakes each actor outside Blender (its Python lacks OpenCV): depth smoothed from the mask interior because edge pixels blend into the background, rim curved back, back shell, bone weights from the mask, joints from mask landmarks. `src/rig.py` builds armatures (root, spine, chest, head, upper arm, forearm; local z toward camera so x nods, y turns, z leans) and sets rotations as whole cycles per loop. `src/scenes.py` uses the clean plate as backdrop and swaps a flow plate per frame; streams use two texture copies carried along flow lines and cross-faded. Forearm swing cut from 6 to 1.5 degrees after a candle travelled 30 px; neck joints lowered on the three large front priests. Renders: seam 0.00, frame step 2.73 and 3.43, heads move 1.1-8.4 GIF px, nods remain weak.
